Plate Redactor

Upload one or more vehicle photos. Plates are auto-detected with a lightweight heuristic and boxed for you — drag to fine-tune, then blur or black out and download, one at a time or all together as a ZIP. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Click to choose images, or drop them here JPG or PNG · multiple files supported · works entirely offline in this tab

Drag a box to reposition it, drag the orange dot at its corner to resize, double-click a box to remove it (when more than one exists). Auto-detect is a heuristic, not a trained model — it works best on clear, mostly front-on shots, and may need a manual nudge on angled or blurry ones.

Processing is done locally on your device using the Canvas API. No images leave your browser.
